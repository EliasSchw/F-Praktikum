\begin{abstract}                                                %Beginn des Abstracts
    \noindent Fourier transform spectroscopy in the near infrared has been performed on doped and undoped GaAs and Si,
     as well as GaSb, to determine the reflectivity and transmittiviy of these materials. 
     At ambient air, without any sample, the transmission spectrum shows strong gas absorption 
     lines for $\text{CO}_2$ and $\text{H}_2\text{O}$. 
     It was also verified that the signal to noise ratio improves with the square root of the number of mirror passes $\sqrt{N_\text{scan}}$.
    The refractive index, extinction and absorption coefficients were determined by evaluating the reflection and transmission spectra.
    The band gap energy and the pulse matrix element were also determined. The band gaps of undoped GaAs $\bandgapGaAsUndoped$, 
    doped GaAs $\bandgapGaAsDoped$, undoped Si $\bandgapSiUndoped$ and doped GaSb $\bandgapGaSbDoped$ were determined. 
    The phonon energy of Si $\OMEGASiUndoped$ was also determined.
