    \noindent Fourier transformation spectroscopy was performed on doped and undoped GaAs and Si, as well as on GaSb,
    to determine optical properties of the mentioned materials. At ambient air, without any sample, the transmission
    spectrum shows strong gas absorption lines for $\text{CO}_2$ and $\text{H}_2\text{O}$. Furthermore, it
    could be verified that the signal to noise ratio improves with the square root of the number of mirror passes $\sqrt{N}$.
    The refractive index, extinction and absorption coefficient were determined by evaluating the reflection and transmission spectra.
    The bandgap energy and pulse matrix element were also determined. The bandgaps of undoped GaAs $\bandgapGaAsUndoped$,
    doped GaAs $\bandgapGaAsDoped$, undoped Si ??macro??, doped Si ??macro?? and doped GaSb $\bandgapGaSbDoped$
    were determined. The phonon energy of Si $\hquerOMEGASiUndoped$ was determined additionally.
