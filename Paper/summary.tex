\section{Summary}
Fourier transform spectroscopy has been carried out on doped and undoped GaAs and Si, as well as GaSb.
The aim was to investigate the optical properties of these materials in the near infrared.
As all samples were measured in ambient air, the influence of the atmosphere on the spectra was 
investigated and several gas absorption lines were identified. Mostly $\text{CO}_2$ and $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ 
absorptions influence the spectra. The signal-to-noise ratio for different numbers of mirror passes was also 
investigated. The theoretical prediction of an improvement of the signal according to $\sqrt{N}$ was confirmed. 
All measurements were made in the spectral range from $1500\,\text{nm}$ to $15000\text{nm}$.
First, a high resolution transmission measurement was made to determine the refractive index of the samples 
by averaging over the distance between the transmission peaks. For lower resolution, the refractive index, 
extinction and absorption coefficients were determined by evaluating the reflection and transmission spectra.
The bandgap energy and the pulse matrix element were also determined from these spectra. 
The bandgaps of undoped GaAs $\bandgapGaAsUndoped$, doped GaAs $\bandgapGaAsDoped$, undoped Si $\bandgapSiUndoped$ and doped GaSb $\bandgapGaSbDoped$ were determined and show good agreement with 
literature values. For Si the phonon energy was additionally determined.
