\section{Summary}
Fourier transformation spectroscopy was performed on doped and undoped GaAs and Si, as well as on GaSb.
The goal was to investigate the optical properties of the mentiond materials in the near infrared range.
Because all samples are measured at ambient air, the influence of the atmosphere on the spectra was investigated
and several gas absorption lines were identified. Furthermore, the signal to noise ratio for different numbers
of mirror passes was investigated. The theoretical prediction of a improvement of the signal according to
$\sqrt{N}$ was confirmed. All measurements were done in the spectal range of $1500\,\text{nm}$ to $15000\text{nm}$.
At first a transmission measurement was done at high resolution to determine the refractive index of the 
samples by averaging over the distance between the transmission oscillations. For lower resolution, the
refractive index, extiction and absorption coefficient were determined by evaluating the reflection and transmission spectra.
From those spectra, teh bandgap energy and pulse matix element were also determined. The Bandgaps of undoped GaAs 
$\bandgap_GaAs_Undoped$, doped GaAs $\bandgap_GaAs_Doped$, undoped Si $\bandgap_Si_Undoped$, doped Si $\bandgap_Si_Doped$ 
and doped GaSb $\bandgap_GaSb$ were determined and show a good agreement with literature values. For Si the phonon
energy was determined additionally.
